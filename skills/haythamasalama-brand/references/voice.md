# Voice

**Read when:** you write any text in his name, in English or Arabic: a page, a caption, a post, a bio.
**Owns:** the voice, the rules for English and Arabic, the words to avoid, the bios, and the honesty rules.

His readers include CEOs, CTOs, engineers and recruiters; write so all of them follow. Name, title and handle are
in [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Checked 2026-11-03 against the site's brand page.

## TL;DR

- **Plain words over jargon. Show the work before the title. Warm, never loud.**
- Short sentences, one idea per paragraph, first person for his own work.
- No marketing words and no claim that can't be traced to code, history or a measurement.
- English: British spelling in new text, sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Arabic: simple Modern Standard Arabic, technical names in Latin letters, Latin digits.

## Rules for both languages

- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- First person for his own work ("I designed and built..."). "The team" for shared work.
- If a sentence still works without the adjective, drop the adjective.
- Headings say plainly what the section is ("The import engine", not "One engine to rule them all").
- Technical depth (how it is built, step by step) belongs in articles, not on cards or summaries.

## English

- British spelling in new text: colour, behaviour, organisation, authorise. Don't churn existing pages to change it.
- Sentence case for headings, buttons and running text ("senior software engineer").
- Meta lines use a middle dot with spaces: `Article · Laravel`, `JUL 2023 · laravel/framework #46405`.
- Words to avoid: cutting-edge, seamless, robust, blazing, revolutionary, world-class, leverage, empower, unlock,
  game-changing, next-level, supercharge.
- Filler to avoid: "In today's fast-paced world", "It's worth noting", "Let's dive in", groups of three added for
  rhythm, a closing line that repeats the post.

## Arabic

Register: simple Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى بسيطة), not classical and not dialect. The everyday word over the
literary one, one fixed term per concept.

- His name, handle and domain stay in Latin letters. Ask him before using an Arabic spelling of the name.
- Technical names stay in Latin letters (Laravel, API, Nuxt).
- Numbers in Latin digits: 30 يوماً، 6 ساعات.
- First person for his own work: «صممتُ وبنيتُ ...». Shared work: «الفريق».
- Words to avoid: ثوري، لا مثيل له، الأفضل على الإطلاق، سلس تماماً، قوي جداً، نقلة نوعية، متكامل وشامل.
- The tone, by example:
  - «أنشئ الفاتورة وأرسلها إلى العميل في خطوة واحدة. تبقى نسخة منها في حسابك.»
  - Not: «منصة ثورية متكاملة تمنحك تجربة سلسة لا مثيل لها في إدارة فواتيرك.»

## Bios

Bios are in the third person; posts and his own pages use the first person.

Short bio (about 40 words):

> Haytham A. Salama is a senior software engineer who builds SaaS, logistics and fintech platforms. He contributes
> to Nuxt UI, writes about shipping software, and works every day alongside AI coding agents.

Long bio (about 80 words):

> Haytham A. Salama is a senior software engineer who has been shipping software since 2016. He designs systems
> around the domain, builds the APIs, integrations and security they run on, and leads small teams that take them to
> production. Before code, he repaired electronics and worked as a designer and video editor, which still shapes how
> his software feels. He has contributed to Laravel, Nuxt and Nuxt UI, and works every day alongside AI coding agents.

## Honesty

- Every claim can be traced to code, git history or a measurement. If it can't, ask him; don't invent it.
- Say exactly what he did: "I designed and built X", "I built the foundation of X; the team grew it".
- No unmeasured outcomes ("3× faster" only when it was measured).
- Nothing internal to an employer or a client: no secrets, internal numbers, screenshots or code.
